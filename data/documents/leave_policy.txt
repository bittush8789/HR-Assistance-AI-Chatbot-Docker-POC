DOCUMENT: LEAVE POLICY (POLICY REF: HR-POL-001)
EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2026
VERSION: 2.1
APPLICABILITY: ALL FULL-TIME AND PART-TIME REGULAR EMPLOYEES

1. OVERVIEW
This policy outlines the entitlements, guidelines, and procedures for taking various types of leave at the company. All leave requests are handled through the internal HR portal.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE (PAID TIME OFF)
- Entitlement: Full-time employees accrue 20 paid annual leave days per calendar year (accrued at approximately 1.67 days per full month worked).
- Part-time employees receive prorated leave based on their contracted weekly hours.
- Carryover: A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the following calendar year. Any carried-over days must be used by March 31st of the new year, or they will lapse.
- Advance Notice: For planned leave of 3 or more consecutive business days, employees must submit requests at least two (2) weeks in advance. For 1-2 days, at least 48 hours notice is required.
- Approval: Subject to direct manager approval based on operational needs.

3. SICK AND MEDICAL LEAVE
- Entitlement: Full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, allocated on January 1st.
- Medical Documentation: If sick leave extends beyond three (3) consecutive business days, a certified medical certificate from a licensed healthcare provider must be uploaded to the HR portal upon return.
- Notification: Employees must inform their line manager by 9:00 AM on the day of absence.

4. PARENTAL LEAVE
- Maternity Leave: Eligible primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave following birth or adoption.
- Paternity and Partner Leave: Eligible secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken within the first 12 months of the child's arrival.
- Eligibility Requirement: Employees must have completed at least six (6) continuous months of employment prior to the expected date of delivery or adoption.

5. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
- Immediate Family: Up to five (5) paid business days for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- Extended Family: Up to three (3) paid business days for the loss of grandparents, in-laws, or domestic partners' relatives.

6. COMPASSIONATE AND UNPAID LEAVE
- Unpaid leave of up to thirty (30) days may be requested for personal emergencies or extended caregiving.
- Approval requires joint sign-off from the Department Head and the HR Director.

7. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
- The company observes 11 standard paid public holidays annually. A schedule is published on the intranet at the start of each calendar year.

8. CONTACT
For leave questions or balance disputes, contact the People Operations Team at hr-leaves@company.local.
