DOCUMENT: WORK FROM HOME AND HYBRID WORK POLICY (POLICY REF: HR-POL-002)
EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2026
VERSION: 3.0
APPLICABILITY: ALL REGULAR FULL-TIME EMPLOYEES

1. PURPOSE
The Work from Home (WFH) policy provides a flexible work framework that balances individual flexibility with in-person team collaboration and operational excellence.

2. ELIGIBILITY AND SCHEDULE
- Eligibility: Employees who have completed their initial 90-day probationary period and are in good performance standing are eligible for hybrid remote work.
- Hybrid Model: Employees may work remotely up to three (3) days per week.
- Anchor Collaboration Days: Tuesdays and Thursdays are designated mandatory in-office collaboration days for all hybrid teams.
- Fully Remote Exceptions: Fully remote arrangements require formal approval from the VP of the respective business unit and the HR Director.

3. CORE WORKING HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
- Core Business Hours: All employees must be accessible and responsive via Slack, email, and Google Meet between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time.
- Status Updates: Employees must update their Slack status and calendar indicating their remote or office working status daily.

4. HOME OFFICE SETUP STIPEND
- Initial Allowance: A one-time, non-taxable stipend of $500 USD is provided to eligible employees upon joining or transitioning to hybrid work.
- Permitted Expenses: Ergonomic desk chair, secondary computer monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset, and desk risers.
- Claims: Claims must be submitted via the company Expense Portal with itemized receipts within 60 days of purchase.

5. EQUIPMENT AND IT SECURITY
- Company Hardware: The company issues a standardized laptop, power adapters, and peripherals. Personal laptops are strictly prohibited for work-related data access without IT approval.
- High-Speed Internet: Employees must maintain a reliable home broadband internet connection with a minimum download speed of 50 Mbps and upload speed of 10 Mbps.
- Virtual Private Network (VPN): Mandatory connection to the company GlobalProtect VPN is required whenever accessing production clusters, internal tools, and client documents.
- Public Wi-Fi: Working from public Wi-Fi networks (cafes, airports, hotels) is prohibited unless connected via the company VPN.

6. ERGONOMICS AND HEALTH & SAFETY
- Employees must maintain a dedicated, secure, and ergonomic workspace free from hazards and distractions.
- Workplace injuries occurring during official working hours in the designated home workspace must be reported to HR within 24 hours.

7. CONTACT
Questions regarding WFH requests or schedule adjustments should be sent to hr-flexwork@company.local.
